FAQ

Questions, answered.

Pricing, measuring, the fit guarantee, shipping, returns, installation and texting.

Pricing

How much do shades cost?

From $95 a shade. Your price depends on the size, style, fabric and options. You get the exact price by text after you measure and our team checks your numbers. The quote shows the full price, including any shipping and sales tax, before you pay.

Why do you cost less than a high-end shade studio?

We sell direct, online and by text, and you measure and hang your own shades with our help. That's how we offer the look and design help of a high-end shade studio at about a third of the price.

When do I pay?

Up front. Once you approve the summary of your windows, you pay the full order price with a secure payment link (processed by Stripe). We never ask for your card number by text.

Measuring and the fit guarantee

Do I measure my own windows?

Yes, with our guided measuring form, which walks you through each window. Give us the exact opening and don't subtract anything; we make the adjustments. Then a person on our team checks every number and photo. The measuring guide covers it step by step.

What if I measure wrong?

That's what our check is for. If a number looks off against your photos, we'll ask you to re-measure just that window. And if we checked your measurements and a shade still doesn't fit, we remake it free.

What does the fit guarantee cover?

If our team checked your measurements before you paid and a shade doesn't fit, we remake it free, including shipping to you. One remake per window; the remake can change the size and the mount, in the same product, fabric and color. It doesn't cover windows we didn't check or a change of mind about fabric or style. Details are in our terms of sale.

Inside or outside mount?

Inside the frame looks clean and built-in. Outside, on the wall, blocks the most light and makes the window look bigger. The measuring guide helps you choose, and we confirm when we check your numbers.

Orders, shipping and returns

How long does it take?

Shades ship 2 to 4 weeks after you pay, plus time in transit. We text you when your shades ship, with tracking.

Can I return a shade?

Not for a change of mind. Each shade is cut and built to your measurements, so we can't take it back if you change your mind about fabric, color, style or mount. That's why we offer a $9 swatch kit and check your measurements before you buy.

If a shade arrives damaged or defective, or doesn't match your order, we repair or replace it free. If it doesn't fit after our check, we remake it free.

Can I cancel?

Yes, for a full refund, until we send your order to production. Text us as soon as you can.

Can I see the fabric first?

Yes. Our swatch kit is $9, mailed to you, and the $9 is credited to your shade order.

Installation

Do you install?

No. Each shade hangs on two brackets with a few screws, about 15 to 20 minutes a shade. Instructions come with your shades, and any handyman can do it.

Texting and Dana

Who is Dana?

Dana is Tailored Light's AI design assistant. Dana helps by text with style, fabric, measuring and your quote, and a person on our team reviews every text.

What will you text me about?

Only your design, measurements, quote and order, and only after you text us first or give us your number. Reply STOP anytime and we'll stop. We never share your number for marketing. See our texting terms and privacy policy.

Do I need an app?

No. The room studio runs in your browser, and everything else happens by text.
