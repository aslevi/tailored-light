FAQ

Do I need someone to come to my house?

No. Try shades on here, then text a designer. We'll help you pick, measure and order, all by text.

How much do shades cost?

From $209 a shade: roller from $209, zebra from $239, Roman from $259, woven from $269. Add your window size on any shade to see your price. Each window is priced separately. Zebra prices are an estimate until we confirm by text.

Why is it less than an in-home studio?

No showroom and no home visits. That keeps prices at about half of in-home studio prices.

Is measuring hard?

Not really. About 7 minutes with a regular tape: a window photo, three widths and three heights. We do the math, and Dana checks every number. Measure wrong? We'll remake it free. Start the measuring guide

Can I see the fabric first?

Yes. Pick up to 5 fabrics and we'll mail free samples.

How long does it take?

Shades ship 2–4 weeks after you order. We'll text you tracking.

Can I return a shade?

Custom shades can't be returned for a change of mind. But if you measured wrong, we'll remake it free. If something's wrong with it, we'll fix or replace it.

Do you install?

No, and you probably won't need help. Each shade hangs in about 15–20 minutes with two brackets, a few screws and a drill. Everything you need is in the box except the drill.

Who will I be texting?

A designer on our team, like Dana. We usually reply within minutes. Reply STOP anytime and we'll stop.

Something else?

Text a designer. We're happy to help.
