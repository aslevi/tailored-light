Try onText a designer

We'll guide you. About 5 minutes.

A metal tape and your phone. Dana checks every number before anything is made.

Start measuringText a designer
  1. 1

    Snap the tape

    Two photos per window with your phone: the tape across the width, and the tape from the top down to the sill. Get the numbers in the shot. You'll attach them in Messages.

  2. 2

    One width, one height

    Inside the frame, wood to wood, to the nearest 1/8". Don't subtract anything; we make the adjustments.

  3. 3

    Send. Dana checks it.

    Dana reads your numbers and photos and recommends inside or outside mount for you, so there's nothing to choose.

Measure wrong? We'll remake it free.

If something looks off, we'll ask you to re-measure just that window. One free remake per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when you measured with our guide and sent us your numbers and photos first.
