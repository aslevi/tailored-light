About

Beautiful shades shouldn't need a showroom.

Tailored Light is a small team that makes custom window shades and helps you choose them by text. The idea is simple: the look and design help of a high-end shade studio, at about a third of the price.

How we do it

  • An edited collection. Roller, Roman and zebra shades in fabrics we've chosen, not a catalog of thousands.
  • Made to measure. Every shade is built to your window.
  • You measure, we check. Our guided form makes measuring simple, and a person on our team checks every number and photo.
  • Design help by text. Dana, our AI design assistant, helps you choose. A person on our team reviews every text.
  • Honest about what we don't do. We don't install (each shade hangs in 15–20 minutes), and we can't take returns for a change of mind on custom shades. We check your measurements and send free swatches so you get it right the first time.
