How it works

Designer shades, without the showroom.

You design online, measure with our guided form, and a person on our team checks everything before your shades are made.

  1. Design your room

    Try roller, Roman and zebra shades in every fabric on our sample rooms, then send your design to Dana. Or skip the studio and text a photo of your window.

    Open the room studio

  2. See the real fabric

    Order a swatch kit for $9. It's mailed to you, and the $9 is credited to your shade order.

    About the swatch kit

  3. Measure your windows

    Our guided measuring form walks you through each window: inside or outside, the numbers, and three photos. Give us the exact opening and don't subtract anything; we make the adjustments.

    Read the measuring guide

  4. We check every number

    A person on our team checks your numbers against your photos. If one looks off, we'll ask you to re-measure just that window. When everything checks out, we confirm in writing by text.

  5. Get your price and pay

    We text you a summary of each window and the exact price, including shipping and tax. Reply YES and pay up front with a secure payment link. Nothing is made until you've done both.

  6. Made, shipped, hung

    Shades ship in 2 to 4 weeks, and we text you when they ship. We don't install: each shade hangs on two brackets with a few screws, about 15 to 20 minutes a shade. Any handyman can do it.

Fit guarantee

Checked by us, guaranteed to fit.

If our team checked your measurements and a shade doesn't fit, we remake it free. That includes shipping, one remake per window, and the remake can change the size or the mount. It covers the windows our team checked before you paid.

Read the full terms

Good to know

  • Paid up front. You pay the full order price before production starts.
  • No returns for a change of mind. Every shade is custom, so order the swatch kit if you're unsure about a color.
  • Damaged or defective? Text us photos and we repair or replace it at no cost.
  • Changed your mind early? You can cancel for a full refund until your order goes to production.
