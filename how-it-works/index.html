Good to know

Measuring

About 7 minutes with a regular tape. We do the math and check every number. How to measure

Paying

Add your window size to see your price. When you're ready, we text you a summary and a secure payment link.

Returns

Custom shades can't be returned for a change of mind. Here's what we cover:

  • Measure wrong? We'll remake it free.
  • Something's wrong with it? We'll fix or replace it.
  • Damaged in shipping? We'll send a new one.

Hanging your shades
