Try onText a designer

You don't need a home visit.

Just a little guidance. Here's how it works.

  1. Try it on

    See real shades in a room. Pick a type, a fabric and a color.

  2. Text a designer

    Send a photo of your window or a look you like. We'll help you pick, measure and order.

  3. We make it and ship it

    Made for your window and shipped in 2–4 weeks. It hangs in about 15–20 minutes.

Fit guarantee

Measure wrong? We'll remake it free. One free remake per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when you measured with our guide and sent us your numbers and photos first.

Good to know

Measuring

It takes about 10 minutes, and we check every number. How to measure

Paying

Add your window size and you'll see your price. When you're ready, we text you a summary and a secure payment link.

Returns

Custom shades can't be returned for a change of mind. Here's what we cover:

  • Measure wrong? We'll remake it free.
  • Something's wrong with it? We'll fix or replace it.
  • Damaged in shipping? We'll send a new one.

Hanging your shades

About 15–20 minutes a shade: two brackets, a few screws, a drill.

In the box

  • Your shade
  • Two brackets
  • Screws
  • Instructions

You'll need

  • A drill
  • A pencil
  • A tape measure
