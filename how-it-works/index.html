How it works

Studio design help, without the showroom.

You see real shades in a room, get design help by text, measure with our guide, and a person on our team checks every number before your shades are made.

What happens next

  1. DesignTry shades in the room studio, or text a photo.
  2. Free swatchesUp to 5 fabrics, mailed free.
  3. Measure with our helpAbout 10 minutes. We check every number.
  4. Made & shippedIn 2–4 weeks. Hangs in 15–20 minutes.

  1. Design your room

    Try roller, Roman and zebra shades in every fabric on our sample rooms, then send your design to Dana. Or skip the studio and text a photo of your window.

    Open the room studio

  2. Get free swatches

    Pick up to 5 fabrics and we mail them free, so you can see the real color in your own light.

    Get free swatches

  3. Measure with our help

    Our guided form takes about 10 minutes: inside or outside, the numbers, and three photos a window. Give us the exact opening and don't subtract anything; we make the adjustments. Stuck? Text Dana and we'll walk you through it, or set up a quick FaceTime.

    Read the measuring guide

  4. We check every number

    A person on our team checks your numbers against your photos. If one looks off, we'll ask you to re-measure just that window. When everything checks out, we confirm in writing by text.

  5. Get your price and pay

    We text you a summary of each window and the exact price, including shipping and tax. Reply YES and pay up front with a secure payment link. Nothing is made until you've done both.

  6. Made, shipped, hung

    Shades ship in 2–4 weeks, and we text you when they ship. Each shade hangs in 15–20 minutes: two brackets, a few screws, a drill. Any handyman can do it too.

Fit guarantee

Measure wrong? We remake it free.

One free remake per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when you measured with our guide and sent us your numbers and photos before ordering. We check every set, and that check is why we can promise it.

Read the full terms

Returns, answered

Custom shades can't be returned for a change of mind. Here's what we cover:

  • Measure wrong? We remake it free (one remake per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when we checked your numbers and photos before you ordered).
  • Defects in materials or workmanship: repaired or replaced free.
  • Damage in shipping: replaced free. Text us photos.

Installing

Hangs in 15–20 minutes a shade: two brackets, a few screws, a drill.

  • Instructions

You'll need

  • A pencil
  • A tape measure (a level helps)
