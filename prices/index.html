How much do custom shades cost?

Every price, by type and window size.

Hello Shade custom shades cost $209 to about $1,499 each, depending on type and size. For a common 36×60″ window: Roller $229, Zebra $259 (estimate), Roman $329 ($359 with a room-darkening lining) and Woven $399. Each window is priced separately. That's about half of in-home studio prices, because there's no showroom and no home visit.

  • Made to measure: 12–96″ wide, 12–120″ high. Ships 2–4 weeks after you order.
  • Samples: up to 5 fabrics for $10, credited to your shade order.
  • Fit guarantee: measure wrong and we remake it free, once per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when a designer checked your numbers and photos first.

What sets the price

  • Type: Roller costs least, then Zebra, Roman and Woven.
  • Size: width × height. Bigger windows cost more.
  • Lining: a room-darkening lining adds a little to a Roman.
  • Fabric and color don’t change the price within a type.

Questions about price

Why isn’t my shade $209?

$209 is the smallest Roller, 24×24″. Size and type set the price; fabric and color within a type don’t.

Is the price shown the price I pay?

The price shown for your size is held unless our measurement check finds a different size. Your written quote shows the full price, including any shipping and sales tax, before you pay.

Do you offer discounts?

Prices are the same for everyone. To lower the price, try another type, skip the lining or start with fewer windows.

How do I get an exact price?

Add your window width and height on any shade page under Your price. Sizes outside 12–96″ wide or 12–120″ high are quoted by text.
