How much do custom Woven shades cost?

Woven shades are natural woven wood with a flat fold. Here’s every price by window size.

Hello Shade custom Woven shades cost $269 for a small 24×24″ window, $399 for a common 36×60″ window and $1,249 for a large 96×96″ window. Price is set by width × height. Each shade is made to measure and ships 2–4 weeks after you order. In-home studios typically charge about twice as much for a similar shade, because the price includes a showroom and a home visit.

  • Fabrics: Natural Reed.
  • Made to measure: 12–96″ wide, 12–120″ high. Ships 2–4 weeks after you order.
  • Samples: up to 5 fabrics for $10, credited to your shade order.
  • Fit guarantee: measure wrong and we remake it free, once per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when a designer checked your numbers and photos first.

Questions

Why are Woven shades priced by size?

Each shade is made for one window, so the price follows width × height. Fabric and color don’t change the price within a type.

How do I get my exact price?

Add your window size under Your price on any Woven shade. Sizes from 12–96″ wide and 12–120″ high get an instant price; others are quoted by text.

Does the price include installation?

No. You hang it yourself in about 15–20 minutes with two brackets, a few screws and a drill. That’s part of why it costs less than an in-home studio.
