How it works

Four steps to your window.

The full process

  1. Design

    Try every style and fabric in the room studio, or text us a photo of your window.

  2. Swatches

    Hold the real fabric up to your window. The $9 kit is credited to your order.

  3. Measure, we check

    Our guided form walks you through it. A person on our team checks every number and photo.

  4. Made & shipped

    Made to your measurements and shipped in 2–4 weeks. Each shade hangs in about 15–20 minutes.

Design help by text

A designer by text, not a configurator.

Send your design from the studio, a photo of your window, or just a question. Dana, Tailored Light's AI design assistant, helps with style, fabric, measuring and your quote, and a person on our team reviews every text.

Fit guarantee

You measure. We check. It fits.

Send us the exact opening and three photos per window. A person on our team checks every number before anything is made. If we checked your measurements and a shade doesn't fit, we remake it free.
