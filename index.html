How it works

From first idea to your window.

  1. Design

    Try styles and fabrics in the room studio, or text Dana a photo of your window.

  2. Feel the fabric

    Order a $9 swatch kit by mail. The $9 is credited to your order.

  3. Measure

    Our guided form walks you through it, window by window.

  4. We check

    A person on our team checks every number and photo before anything is made.

  5. Pay

    Your exact price comes by text. You pay up front with a secure link.

  6. It ships

    Shades ship in 2 to 4 weeks. Two brackets and a few screws to hang.

Questions

Good to know

How much do shades cost?

From $95 a shade. Your price depends on size, style, fabric and options. You get the exact price by text after you measure, before you pay anything.

Do you install?

No. Each shade hangs on two brackets with a few screws, about 15 to 20 minutes a shade. Any handyman can do it.

Can I return a shade?

Every shade is made to your measurements, so we can't take returns for a change of mind. That's why we offer the swatch kit and check your measurements first. If a shade arrives damaged or defective, or doesn't fit after our check, we fix or remake it free.
