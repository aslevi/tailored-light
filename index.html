What is Hello Shade?

Hello Shade sells custom window shades online: Roller, Roman, Zebra and Woven, made to measure for each window and shipped to your door 2–4 weeks after you order. There's no home visit. You try shades on in a room on the site, then text a designer who helps you pick, measure and order. Shades start at $209; a 36×60″ Roller is $229. Each one hangs in about 15–20 minutes with a drill.

  • From prices: Roller $209, Zebra $239 (estimate), Roman $259, Woven $269.
  • Made to measure: 12–96″ wide, 12–120″ high. Ships 2–4 weeks after you order.
  • Samples: up to 5 fabrics for $10, credited to your shade order.
  • Fit guarantee: measure wrong and we remake it free, once per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when a designer checked your numbers and photos first.
  • Text a designer: (201) 745-3300.
