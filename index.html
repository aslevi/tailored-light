Good questions.

Something else? See all or text a designer.

Do I need someone to come to my house?

No. Try shades on here, then text a designer. We'll help you pick, measure and order, all by text.

How much do shades cost?

From $209 a shade: Roller from $209, Zebra from $239, Roman from $259, Woven from $269. Add your window size on any shade to see your price. Each window is priced separately. Zebra prices are an estimate until we confirm by text.

Is measuring hard?

Not really. All you need is a regular metal tape: a window photo, three widths and three heights. We do the math, and Dana checks every number. Measure wrong? We'll remake it free. Start the measuring guide

Can I see the fabric first?

Yes. Pick up to 5 fabrics and we'll mail you samples for $10, credited to your order.

How long does it take?

Shades are at your house in 2–3 weeks. We'll text you tracking.

Do you install?

No, and you probably won't need help. Each shade hangs with two brackets, a few screws and a drill. Everything you need is in the box except the drill.

What is Hello Shade?

Hello Shade sells custom window shades online: Roller, Roman, Zebra and Woven, made to measure for each window and at your house in 2–3 weeks. There's no home visit. You try shades on in a room on the site, then text a designer who helps you pick, measure and order. Shades start at $209; a 36×60″ Roller is $229. Each one hangs with two brackets and a drill.

  • From prices: Roller $209, Zebra $239 (estimate), Roman $259, Woven $269.
  • Made to measure: 12–96″ wide, 12–120″ high. At your house in 2–3 weeks.
  • Samples: up to 5 fabrics for $10, credited to your shade order.
  • Fit guarantee: measure wrong and we remake it free, once per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when a designer checked your numbers and photos first.
