About this fabric

Blackout Linen is a blackout fabric with a linen texture, made for our roman shades, in 1 color: Cream.

Colors vary from screen to screen. The $9 swatch kit shows the real fabric.

Light & privacy

A blackout fabric, made for bedrooms and nurseries. For the darkest room, choose an outside mount: light can sneak around the edges of a shade mounted inside the frame.

Measuring

You measure with our guided form: give us the exact opening and don't subtract anything; we make the adjustments. A person on our team checks every number and photo. Read the measuring guide or open the measuring form.

Fit guarantee

If our team checked your measurements before you paid and a shade doesn't fit, we remake it free, including shipping. One remake per window. Details

Production & shipping

Paid up front. Shades ship 2–4 weeks after payment, and we text you tracking. Custom shades can't be returned for a change of mind; damaged or defective shades are repaired or replaced free.

Installation

We don't install. Each shade hangs on two brackets with a few screws, about 15–20 minutes a shade. Any handyman can do it.
