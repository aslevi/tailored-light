About this fabric

Ticking Stripe is a light filtering fabric with a linen texture, in Cream & Fog. Screens vary, so free samples show the real color.

Light

Light filtering. Add a room-darkening lining for a darker room.

How to measure

About 5 minutes: two tape photos, one width and one height. We check every number. How to measure

Fit guarantee

Measure wrong? We'll remake it free. One free remake per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when you measured with our guide and sent us your numbers and photos first.

Returns

Custom shades can't be returned for a change of mind. Here's what we cover:

  • Measure wrong? We'll remake it free.
  • Something's wrong with it? We'll fix or replace it.
  • Damaged in shipping? We'll send a new one.
Shipping

Ships 2–4 weeks after you order. We'll text you tracking.
