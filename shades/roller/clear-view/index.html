About this fabric

Clear View is a light filtering fabric with a textured weave, in White, Mist, Slate. Screens vary, so samples show the real color.

Light

How to measure

About 7 minutes with a regular tape: a window photo, three widths and three heights. We do the math and check every number. How to measure

Fit guarantee

Measure wrong? We'll remake it free. One free remake per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when you measured with our guide and sent us your numbers and photos first.

Returns

Custom shades can't be returned for a change of mind. Here's what we cover:
