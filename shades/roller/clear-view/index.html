About this fabric

Fine solar screen. Cuts glare, keeps the view. Solar screen, 5% open, in Pure White, Sand, White Grey, Dark Grey. Screens vary, so samples show the real color.

Light

Cuts glare and heat while you keep the view out. By day it's private from outside; at night with lights on, people can see in. Lower openness means more privacy and less view.

How to measure

All you need is a regular metal tape: a window photo, three widths and three heights. We do the math and check every number. How to measure

Fit guarantee

Measure wrong? We'll remake it free. One free remake per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when you measured with our guide and sent us your numbers and photos first.

Returns

Custom shades can't be returned for a change of mind. Here's what we cover:
