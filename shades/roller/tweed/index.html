About this fabric

Fine tweed weave with depth. Blackout, in Almond, Marshmallow, Soba, Ash, Steel. Screens vary, so samples show the real color.

Light

Blocks the light, day and night. Best for bedrooms, nurseries and media rooms.

How to measure

All you need is a regular metal tape: a window photo, three widths and three heights. We do the math and check every number. How to measure

Fit guarantee
