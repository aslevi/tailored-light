How much are custom Roller shades?

Hello Shade Roller shades start at $209 (24×24″) and are $229 at 36×60″. Clean and simple. Rolls up tight. Choose from 39 roller fabrics in blackout, light filtering, sheer and solar screen. Light filtering fabrics like Drift give privacy day and night, sheers like Muslin glow softly, solar screens like Clear View keep your view, and blackouts like Canvas block the light.

  • Made to measure: 12–96″ wide, 12–120″ high. At your house in 2–3 weeks.
  • Samples: up to 5 fabrics for $10, credited to your shade order.
  • Fit guarantee: measure wrong and we remake it free, once per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when a designer checked your numbers and photos first.
