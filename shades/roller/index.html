How much are custom Roller shades?

Hello Shade Roller shades start at $209 (24×24″) and are $229 at 36×60″. Clean and simple. Rolls up tight. Choose from Daylight Weave, Morning Haze, Deep Sleep and Clear View. Daylight Weave and Morning Haze filter light, Clear View is a screen weave that keeps your view, and Deep Sleep is blackout.

  • Made to measure: 12–96″ wide, 12–120″ high. Ships 2–4 weeks after you order.
  • Samples: up to 5 fabrics for $10, credited to your shade order.
  • Fit guarantee: measure wrong and we remake it free, once per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when a designer checked your numbers and photos first.
