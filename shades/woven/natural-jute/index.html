About this fabric

Chunky hand-woven jute and hemp, knots and all. Natural woven wood, made as a flat-fold Roman, in Pale Ivory, Creamy White, Warm Oat, Natural Straw, Natural Hemp, Mixed Stripe, Dark Ash. See-through with no liner; add a light-filtering or blackout liner. Natural fibers vary a little from shade to shade, so samples show the real color and texture.

Light

With no liner, daylight and a soft view come through the weave, so it is see-through, especially at night with lights on. A light-filtering liner lets light in but stops the view. A blackout liner stops both.
