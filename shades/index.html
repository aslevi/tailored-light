Clean, simple, and rolls up tight.

A single flat panel of fabric that rolls up out of sight. The most minimal look, and it works almost anywhere: kitchens, offices, bathrooms, behind curtains.

Roman shades

Soft folds, tailored look.

Fabric that gathers into neat horizontal folds as it rises. Warmer and softer than a roller.

Three folds

FlatClean, tailored panel that stacks into neat folds when raised.
HobbledSoft folds cascade all the way down, even when lowered.
RelaxedA gentle curve at the bottom edge. Casual and soft.

Natural Reed, our woven wood, comes in a classic flat fold.

Fabrics
Raw Linen (light filtering), Soft Cotton (light filtering), Ticking Stripe (light filtering), Sheer Voile (light filtering), Blackout Linen (blackout), Natural Reed (woven wood)
Light

Zebra shades

Sheer and solid bands you can align.
