Which shades block the most light?

For a dark bedroom or nursery, Hello Shade's blackout picks are the Canvas Roller (from $209, $229 at 36×60″) and the Blackout Linen Roman (from $259, $329 at 36×60″). Mounted outside the frame, on the wall above the window, a shade blocks the most light because it covers the gaps at the edges.

  • Want it darker than light filtering but softer than blackout? Raw Linen, Soft Cotton and Ticking Stripe Romans can add a room-darkening lining.
  • Made to measure: 12–96″ wide, 12–120″ high. At your house in 2–3 weeks.
  • Samples: up to 5 fabrics for $10, credited to your shade order.
  • Fit guarantee: measure wrong and we remake it free, once per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when a designer checked your numbers and photos first.
