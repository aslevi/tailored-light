Roller, Roman, Zebra or Woven?

How the four shade types compare, and which room each suits.

Pick a Roller for a clean look at the lowest price (from $209), a Roman for soft folds and a tailored look (from $259), a Zebra to adjust light during the day without raising the shade (from $239), or a Woven for natural texture (from $269). For a dark bedroom or nursery, choose a blackout fabric: the Deep Sleep Roller or the Blackout Linen Roman.

Common questions

Roller or Roman?

Roller for a clean, simple look and the lowest price. Roman for soft folds and a finished, tailored look. For a dark bedroom both come in blackout: Deep Sleep Roller or Blackout Linen Roman.

What is a Zebra shade?

Two layers of fabric bands. Slide them to line up the open bands for a view, or the solid bands for privacy, without raising the shade.

Which is darkest?

A blackout fabric (Deep Sleep Roller or Blackout Linen Roman) mounted outside the frame, on the wall above the window.

Which is easiest to clean?

Roller shades: a flat fabric you can wipe or dust, which is why they suit kitchens and bathrooms.
