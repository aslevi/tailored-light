Inside or outside mount?

Where the shade goes, and how to choose.

Quick answerUpdated Oct 30, 2026

An inside mount sits within the window frame for a clean, built-in look; it needs about 2″ of depth for a Roller or Zebra and about 2½″ for a Roman or Woven. An outside mount hangs on the wall above the window and covers the frame, which blocks the most light and works for shallow windows or ones with cranks and handles in the way. For blackout, go outside.

Common questions

How deep does the window need to be for an inside mount?

From the glass to the front edge of the frame: about 2″ for Roller and Zebra, about 2½″ for Roman and Woven.

Which mount is darker?

Outside. Covering the frame and the gaps at the edges blocks the most light, so it’s the pick for a blackout bedroom or nursery.

What if a crank or handle is in the way?

Measure how far it sticks out. If it blocks the shade, mount outside or use a crank extender (about $10 at a hardware store).

Who decides the mount?

You choose, and a designer checks your depth measurement and confirms inside or outside in writing before we make the shade.
