What are the best shades for a bedroom?

For a bedroom that gets really dark, pick a blackout fabric: the Deep Sleep Roller or the Blackout Linen Roman. Mounted outside the frame, on the wall above the window, it blocks the most light. For a softer look, a Raw Linen or Soft Cotton Roman with the room-darkening lining, or a Natural Reed Woven. Prices start at $209; at 36×60″ a Roller is $229 and a Roman $329.

  • Made to measure: 12–96″ wide, 12–120″ high. Ships 2–4 weeks after you order.
  • Samples: up to 5 fabrics for $10, credited to your shade order.
  • Fit guarantee: measure wrong and we remake it free, once per shade, within 30 days of delivery, when a designer checked your numbers and photos first.
