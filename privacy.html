Privacy

Tailored Light · Last updated October 23, 2026

Tailored Light makes custom window shades. This page explains what our website, iPhone app and App Clip collect and why.

The app and App Clip

  • We use your camera only to show shades on your window, live, on your phone. Camera images are processed on your device and are never uploaded or stored by us.
  • The app does not create an account, track you, or show ads.
  • If you tap "Text Dana", your phone's Messages app opens with a message describing the shade you picked. Nothing is sent unless you press send.

The website

  • If you give us your first name and mobile number, we use them only so a designer can text you about your shades. We don't sell or share them.
  • If you order a swatch kit, we use your name, address, email and phone to ship it and send your receipt.
  • Your picks are saved in your browser so you don't have to start over.

Texting

When you text a Tailored Light designer, we keep the conversation to help with your order. Reply STOP at any time and we won't text you again.

Deleting your information

Text us at (201) 745-3300 and we'll delete anything we have about you.
